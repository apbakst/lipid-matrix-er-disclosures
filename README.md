# Extended-Release Pharmaceutical Formulations — Defensive Technical Disclosures

## Defensive publication notice

This repository contains technical disclosures for extended-release oral pharmaceutical formulation methods, dedicated to the public domain. The disclosures establish prior art under 35 U.S.C. § 102(a)(1) effective the dates indicated below.

| Disclosure | Subject | First public disclosure date |
|---|---|---|
| Five extended-release oral minoxidil formulation systems | Elementary Osmotic Pump (EOP), Controlled Porosity Osmotic Pump (CPOP), Multi-Particulate System (MUPS), Lipid Matrix Tablet, and Bi-Layer Push-Pull Osmotic systems with full quantitative compositions, manufacturing processes, and expected release profiles | February 4, 2026 |
| Lipid matrix extended-release pharmaceutical compositions — broad genus disclosure | Comprehensive disclosure of lipid matrix extended-release tablet, capsule, and multiparticulate compositions across glyceryl esters, polyglycolyzed glycerides, fatty acids, fatty alcohols, waxes, hydrogenated oils, sugar esters, polyglyceryl esters, sorbitan esters, phospholipids, sterols, and structured lipids; hot-melt extrusion, melt granulation, spray congealing, melt pelletization, and other processes; full Markush for APIs across hair-loss, dermatology, cardiovascular, CNS, endocrine, anti-infective, oncology, and GI categories; multi-active combinations; 25 enumerated embodiments; 12 worked manufacturing examples | May 4, 2026 |
| Glyceryl behenate (Compritol) lipid matrix with pH-dependent release — supplemental disclosure | Comprehensive disclosure of glyceryl behenate (Compritol)-containing lipid matrix compositions exhibiting pH-dependent dissolution behavior; binary, ternary, and higher-order blends with co-lipids, waxes, fatty acids/alcohols, sugar esters, polyglyceryl esters, sorbitan esters, phospholipids, sterols, polyglycolyzed glycerides, hydrogenated oils; hydrophilic/amphiphilic co-excipient genus; functional and non-functional coating genera; pH-dependent release ratios from ≥1.2 to ≥100; cumulative %-release acceptance ranges from 5 minutes to 24 hours in both acidic and neutral biorelevant media; 18 enumerated embodiments; five worked dissolution profile examples (α–ε) | May 4, 2026 |
| Multi-architectural modified-release oral minoxidil — companion disclosure | Companion to the lipid-matrix disclosures, broadening the public-domain genus to all non-lipid-matrix architectures: multi-particulate systems (pellets, beads, mini-tablets); bilayer and multi-layer tablets; osmotic-pump systems (single-chamber, push-pull, asymmetric-membrane); gastroretentive systems (floating, mucoadhesive, expandable, high-density); chronopharmaceutical / pulsatile systems; hot-melt extrusion-derived solid dispersions, films, and 3D-printed dosage forms; oral suspensions and ready-to-mix powders; combination compositions of minoxidil with 5α-reductase inhibitors, antiandrogens, prostaglandin modulators, JAK inhibitors, Wnt-pathway modulators, and other co-actives; manufacturing processes; pharmacokinetic and dose envelopes | May 6, 2026 |
